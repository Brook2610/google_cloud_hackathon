Our Story

Gai Kitchen brings the beloved Thai street food experience to New York City. Specializing in Hainanese-style chicken and rice, we offer perfectly steamed, roasted, and fried chicken, served with fragrant rice and our signature sauces. Our commitment is to fresh ingredients and authentic flavors.
