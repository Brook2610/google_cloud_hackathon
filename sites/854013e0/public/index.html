Our Story & Specialties

Mei Lai Wah is a beloved institution at 41 Mott St, New York, NY. We specialize in classic Chinese bakery items, most famously our "pineapple" BBQ pork buns and delicious shumai. With a stellar rating of 4.4/5 from over 3,485 reviews, we pride ourselves on offering sweet, savory, and uniquely delicious treats at affordable prices. Whether you're a long-time local or a first-time visitor, we invite you to experience the authentic flavors of Chinatown.
